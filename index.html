Flagship Series

Engineered for the long haul.

Underway builds professional-grade marine and industrial assets for Stormworks: Build and Rescue. Every vessel is designed around real nautical engineering principles — stability calculations, engine room layouts, damage control systems, and authentic operational procedures.

Our catalogue spans expedition yachts, offshore supply vessels, coastal patrol craft, research ships, and the dock infrastructure to support them. Each build ships with full documentation and a live update feed so you're never left behind.
